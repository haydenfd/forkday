# Application profile scope

Forkday saves the common reusable parts of a Workday application in five settings sections. This is a practical selection, not a guarantee of identical forms at every employer: Workday lets employers hide experience sections and configure their questionnaires.

| Section             | Saved content                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Profile             | Legal and preferred name, email, address, US mobile number, LinkedIn, GitHub, website. The required contact fields and fixed US phone control from PR #5 remain.                                                              |
| Resume              | One copied PDF, editable resume text, skills, repeatable roles and education. Roles store company, title, location, month dates, current-role flag, and description; education stores school, degree, field, and month dates. |
| Application answers | US authorization; sponsorship now and in future; US residency/visa status; start date; notice period; relocation; optional salary, currency, and period.                                                                      |
| Disclosures         | Optional gender/race responses and US disability/protected-veteran answers. Unset and “Prefer not to answer” remain distinct.                                                                                                 |
| Codex               | Existing local CLI connection, authentication, and test actions. Invocation history does not appear in settings.                                                                                                              |

The current UI is scoped to US residents applying in the US. Country selection and citizenship questions are omitted; US address and authorization metadata are set internally. Residency/visa status, permission to work, and sponsorship remain separate answers. Nothing is inferred from a name, address, resume, or another answer. Legacy authorization answers saved for another country are shown unset until reviewed. Gender and race/ethnicity remain free text because choices differ by employer. Every application answer and disclosure starts unset.

The PDF is copied immediately after file selection and is independent of the section Save button. Resume text and structured experience are edited manually; PDF text extraction is not implemented. Each section saves independently, preserving the other sections. Contact Save requires core contact fields; resume and answers can be saved before contact setup is complete. Leaving an edited section offers Save changes, Discard, or Keep editing. Dates cannot run backward, and a current role cannot have an end date.

The separate Job queue stores Waiting, Continuing, Completed, Rejected, and Stopped applications with title, company, URL, and notes. Tasks is the home page, showing current work and recent processed records with notes previews. See all opens processed history. These are user-managed local statuses; adding or changing a record does not open a job page or start automation. Status changes request system notifications; native delivery depends on OS permission and, on macOS, app signing. Status summarizes saved contact information, PDF, US authorization answers, Codex health, and application counts. Disclosures are excluded from readiness.

Employer-specific referral questions, previous employment at that employer, relatives, legal attestations, and custom screening questions remain per application. Languages and certifications can be added if actual forms establish a recurring need. Browser opening, account handling, and application automation are outside this change.

## Research sources

- [City of Charlotte external candidate guide, pages 8–10](https://www.charlottenc.gov/files/sharedassets/city/v/2/city-government/departments/documents/hr/workday-guide-for-external-candidates-qrg.pdf): names/contact/address/phone and the experience, education, skills, resume, and website sections.
- [Workday application templates](https://stage.doc.workday.com/admin-guide/en-us/human-capital-management/recruiting/job-applications/ijz1499291475964.html) and [McGill external candidate guide, pages 5–6](https://www.mcgill.ca/tised/files/tised/f_workday_guide_for_external_candidates_0.pdf): configurable experience categories and another employer's use of them.
- [Workday job requisition components](https://doc.workday.com/workday-education/en-us/course-manuals/recruiting-for-administrators/job-requisition-components.html?toc=2): mapped questionnaire concepts including legal eligibility, sponsorship, relocation, start date, notice period, and minimum salary. Inclusion here is a reusable-fields judgment, not a frequency measurement.
- [DOJ technical assistance](https://www.justice.gov/sites/default/files/crt/legacy/2014/04/02/128.pdf): work authorization and sponsorship are distinct from citizenship. This supports separating the data rather than inferring answers.
- [DOL disability self-identification form](https://www.dol.gov/sites/dolgov/files/OFCCP/regs/compliance/sec503/Self_ID_Forms/503Self-IDForm-04262023.pdf), [DOL protected-veteran form](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/ofccp/vevraa/self-id-form), and [Workday candidate personal information](https://stage.doc.workday.com/admin-guide/en-us/human-capital-management/recruiting/candidates/candidate-personal-information/qae1605872030805.html?toc=3.3.10.0): optional US disclosure choices and country-dependent personal-information fields.
